# Study-on-Halfmoon
This task was the part of my "Research Seminar" course at TU Ilmenau, under the [Distributed Systems & Operating Systems Group(DSOS)](https://www.tu-ilmenau.de/universitaet/fakultaeten/fakultaet-informatik-und-automatisierung/profil/institute-und-fachgebiete/institut-fuer-praktische-informatik/fachgebiet-verteilte-systeme-und-betriebssysteme) .

The task was to understand a Research Paper on the Distributed Systems domain, write a report based on your own understanding and present your understanding in the research group for discusssions & answering questions. 

This report was based on the publication ["Halfmoon: Log-Optimal Fault-Tolerant Stateful Serverless Computing"](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3600006.3613154), which was part of the proceedings from [SOSP: ACM Symposium on Operating Systems Principles](https://dl.acm.org/conference/sosp/proceedings).

My Report and presentation was supervised by [Prof. Dr. Boris Koldehofe](https://www.tu-ilmenau.de/universitaet/fakultaeten/fakultaet-informatik-und-automatisierung/profil/institute-und-fachgebiete/institut-fuer-praktische-informatik/fachgebiet-verteilte-systeme-und-betriebssysteme/team/boris-koldehofe).
